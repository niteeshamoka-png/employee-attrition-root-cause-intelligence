Understanding the problem

Staff turnover is rising. Every exit costs recruiting fees, lost productivity and team disruption, and usually repeats because nobody knows why people leave. Exit interviews are late and biased; HR holds the real signals in separate systems.

What the system must do

  • Integrate performance, workload, pay, absenteeism, overtime, engagement and training data.
  • Diagnose which organisational factors are associated with leaving, by department.
  • Predict who is at risk so managers can act early.
  • Recommend retention interventions backed by evidence and a cost-benefit case.

Important caution

The dashboard shows association, not proof of cause. Before rolling out a costly action, pilot it and compare against a control group. Risk scores should guide conversations, never automatic decisions about individuals.

Architecture (maps to course sessions 4, 11, 12, 14)

Orchestrator agent, then Data Agent (cleans and joins), Diagnostic Agent (drivers), Risk Agent (scores), Intervention Agent (simulates and ranks), and a human approval gate for governance.
